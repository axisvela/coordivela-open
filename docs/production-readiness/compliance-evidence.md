# Compliance Evidence And Acceptance Feedback

## Context And Boundary

EE 1.0.0 is not a compliance certification or independent audit opinion. This
public issue requests input on evidence formats and acceptance criteria only.
Enterprise compliance/governance implementation stays in EE; community evidence
format and public runbook improvements may belong in Core/Open.

## Evidence Still Needed

- Define the applicable framework/control scope and responsible owners before
  making compliance claims; do not infer controls from a feature checklist.
- Map identity, least privilege, tenant isolation, approval, audit, privacy,
  recovery, release/change management and incident handling to attributable
  evidence with dates, revisions, tests, limits and accepted risks.
- Distinguish a local fixture, integration test, deployed pilot and production
  acceptance. Capture failed attempts, cleanup and retention gaps explicitly.
- Review redaction, evidence retention/access, data lifecycle and audit export.
- Obtain named security/compliance/operations approvals in the authorized
  private process; never request confidential audit reports in public comments.

## Community Feedback Requested

Which non-confidential control mappings or evidence schemas help you evaluate
adoption? Share blank templates, public references or sanitized gaps rather
than customer evidence, personal data or private reports.

## Closure Criteria

- A scoped evidence mapping and exceptions register are reviewed by the owners.
- Public evidence claims have accurate provenance and reproducible boundaries.
- Required owner approvals exist; issue closure or a 1.0 tag is not certification.
